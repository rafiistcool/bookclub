**First pass, 2026-10-29. This is a desk check, not legal clearance.** It shows which names deserve a proper trademark search and which ones drop out now. Rafael makes the decision (work plan §4, Q1), and BRAND waits for it.

## 4. Shortlist

1. **Kapitelclub.** `.de`, `.com` and `.app` are all free, and no app has the name. It describes how the clubs read (picks, chapters, milestones). It is German-first, but "club" carries it in English. Before choosing it, search for "Kapitel" and "Chapter" marks in classes 9, 41, 42 and 45.
2. **Shelfmates.** It works in both languages and says "reading together". `.de` is free and `.com` can be bought. Before choosing it, ask the price of the `.com` and run a similarity search against Bookmate and the Shelfmate apps.
3. **Lesebande.** Playful and German-first. `.com` and `.app` are free, and nothing turned up under the name. It is weaker in English and may sound like a children's programme.

My pick is Kapitelclub, because it is the only candidate that is clean on every check so far. If the English reading matters more than the domains, take Shelfmates.

If a German-only name is wanted after all, Eselsohr is the fallback. That means buying the `.com` through Afternic, asking the `.de` holder, and talking to the magazine's publisher first.

## 5. What Rafael still does himself

1. **Choose** one name and a backup from the shortlist, or name another for the same check.
2. **Register the domains** as soon as you choose, before using the name anywhere: `.de`, `.com` and `.app` where they are free. For a taken `.com`, ask its marketplace for the price. Free domains on the shortlist can be gone next week. Holding the free domains of the top two now costs only a few euros a year each. `.app` works only over HTTPS (HSTS preload), which Caddy already handles. To check again first: `curl -s -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code}' https://rdap.denic.de/domain/<name>.de` (404 means free). For the other TLDs, use `https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>.com` and `https://pubapi.registry.google/rdap/domain/<name>.app`.
3. **Run a proper trademark search** before the legal texts (#53), the store listings and open sign-up (#55):
   - Registers: DPMAregister, EUIPO eSearch plus and TMview, with the offices DE, EM and WO.
   - What to search: the exact name and similar spellings, for example `Shelfmate*`, `*mates` and `Bookmate`; `Kapitel*` and `Chapter*club`; `Lesebande` and `*bande`.
   - Nice classes: 9 (downloadable app), 41 (book clubs, online publications), 42 (software as a service) and 45 (online social networking).
   - Company names: check the Handelsregister too, because company names are earlier rights as well (§ 5 MarkenG).
   - For a name that will carry the store apps, a paid similarity search (Ähnlichkeitsrecherche) by a patent attorney is worth the money.
4. **Decide whether to file a mark.** It can wait until things get serious, but the search comes first. A German mark costs 290 EUR at the DPMA online for up to three classes. An EU mark at the EUIPO costs more and covers the whole EU.
5. **Tell me the name.** BRAND then puts it into `frontend/index.html`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `brand.ts`, `en.json` and `de.json`, and `SERVICE_NAME`. The domain also unblocks mail DNS (Brevo), the Impressum, `PUBLIC_URL` and the cutover.
