# Notices about content: intake, decision and removal

How the operator handles notices that content on the service is illegal, infringes someone's rights or breaks the terms: notice and action under Art. 16 and 17 DSA, and the operator's own moderation. It is built from the code; "to confirm" marks Rafael's open decisions. Have #53's legal source confirm the DSA wording (work plan Q12).

`server/tests/legal_docs.rs` checks that every endpoint this file names exists in the server's OpenAPI document, or is in its list of planned endpoints, and that every app file it names exists.

## 1. Targets (to confirm, Q12)

| Step | Target (recommended option A) |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge a notice received by mail | within 24 hours |
| Decide a notice | within 72 hours of receipt |
| Tell the reporter and the uploader | when deciding: the app mails both after the decision (`server/src/admin/notices.rs` (`decided`)) |

The admin page flags items open longer than 72 hours (default D4). The app itself promises no time to readers: the report sheet says only that an admin decides and that a reader with a verified address hears the outcome. The 90-day statistics (median and maximum hours to a decision, count over the target) come from `GET /api/admin/summary`; reports closed on arrival (the picture was down already) are left out.

## 2. How notices come in

1. **In the app.** A signed-in user reports with the report sheet (`frontend/src/components/ReportSheet.vue` (`ReportTarget`)):
   - a cover: "Report cover" under the cover on the book page (`frontend/src/pages/BookDetailPage.vue`), which calls `POST /api/media/{public_id}/reports`;
   - a profile picture: "Report profile picture" under the member's name on their shelf page (`frontend/src/pages/MemberShelfPage.vue`), through the same endpoint with the picture's public id. Only another member's picture offers it. The server takes the report when the reporter can see the owner (they share a club; otherwise the answer is 404 like an unknown picture);
   - a book a reader added: "Report this book" beside "Added by a reader" on its book page, which calls `POST /api/books/works/{work_id}/reports`. Books from Open Library, the DNB or the old app offer no report and the server refuses one (`server/src/catalog/reports.rs` (`ONLY_READER_BOOKS`)): they are corrected with an edit request or "Edit as admin" instead;
   - an edition a reader added: "Report this edition" on its correction sheet ("Suggest a correction" beside the reader's edition on the book page, `frontend/src/components/EditRequestSheet.vue`), under "Not a mistake, but offensive or infringing someone's rights?", which calls `POST /api/books/editions/{edition_id}/reports` (`server/src/catalog/reports.rs` (`report_work`, `report_edition`)). The same line offers "Report this book" on the correction sheet of a book a reader added. The edition list itself (`frontend/src/components/EditionPicker.vue`) has no action of its own: it belongs to another package and gets one at integration (to confirm). A reader who has not chosen the edition chooses it first, or writes to the contact address.

   Each report has a reason and details (at most 2,000 characters; 20 new reports a day per account, shared by all kinds; `server/src/media/reports.rs` (`report`, `ReportIn`)). The sheet offers "It shows a different book" (`wrong_book`) for covers only, and "It is offensive" (`offensive`), "It infringes copyright" (`copyright`) and "Something else" (`other`) for every kind. A repeat by the same reader answers with their open report; a report of a picture that is already down is closed at once and the reader is told so. The report lands in the admin queue.
2. **By mail.** Anyone, with or without an account, writes to `CONTACT_EMAIL`, the one public contact and takedown address (default D3). The app shows it in `GET /api/config` (`contact_email`), in the legal section of Settings, on a legal page whose text is not online yet, and at the end of every report sheet: "No account here? Anyone can report content by writing to {email}." (`report.noAccount` in `frontend/src/i18n/en.json`), so a reader can pass it on to someone without an account. The sign-in page shows the same line under its links to the legal texts (`frontend/src/pages/LoginPage.vue`), and people outside the app also find the address in the imprint and the terms, which that page links.
3. **Mail into the queue.** There is no public unauthenticated form (default D2). An instance admin enters a mailed notice about a picture, a cover or a profile picture, with the "Take down a picture by URL" form in the reports tab of the admin page (`frontend/src/components/admin/AdminTakeDown.vue`): the address of the picture (`…/media/{public_id}/l` for a cover, `…/media/{public_id}/a` for a profile picture), of the book page that shows the cover, or the bare picture id. The form shows the picture, then calls `DELETE /api/admin/media/{public_id}` with the body `{details, received_at, reason}`. That takes the picture down and records a resolved report that came in by mail (`via` `notice`), with the time the mail arrived; the history shows it as "Emailed notice". A mailed notice about a book or edition a reader added: the admin files a report of it in the app with the notice in its details (the arrival time too), then decides it; the mail itself goes into the record (§6).

Each notice needs, per Art. 16 (2) DSA: why the content is illegal, where it is (the URL), the name and address of the reporter (except for some offences against children), and a statement of good faith. A notice that lacks them is answered with a request for what is missing.

## 3. Triage

| Reason | Typical content | First question |
|---|---|---|
| Copyright (`copyright`) | a cover photo, a long quote, a pasted review or blurb | Is the reporter the rights holder or acting for them? Is the use covered (cover thumbnails of the book itself, short quotes, § 51 UrhG)? |
| Wrong book (`wrong_book`) | a cover or book data that belongs to another book | A catalog error, not illegal content: fix the record ("Edit as admin"), merge a duplicate, or remove the cover |
| Offensive (`offensive`) | insults, harassment, hate, pictures of people | Is it illegal (for example §§ 130, 185–187 StGB, § 201a StGB, § 22 KUG) or only against the terms? |
| Other (`other`) | personal data of others, spam, anything else | Which law or which clause of the terms does it break? |

Content inside a club is visible to its members only, and personal content to the author's audience (`privacy-facts.md` §10). Covers and books are visible to every signed-in user, and a profile picture to everyone who shares a club with its owner, so most notices will be about them.

## 4. Removal per kind of content

| Content | Who can remove it today | How |
|---|---|---|
| Cover photo a user uploaded | instance admin | From the report: "Take down", then "Take down for good", in the reports tab of the admin page (`frontend/src/components/admin/AdminReports.vue`), which calls `POST /api/admin/reports/{report_id}/resolve` with `{"action": "remove"}`. Without a report: the "Take down a picture by URL" form (§2), or `DELETE /api/admin/media/{public_id}`. Either way the files are deleted, the hashes of the original and every variant are blocked so the same picture cannot come back, every book that showed it loses the cover, the picture's open reports are resolved, and `/media/{public_id}/…` answers 410 (`server/src/media/reports.rs` (`take_down_locked`, `record_removed_hashes`)). A browser that loaded the picture before can still show its cached copy for up to a year (`server/src/media/serve.rs` (`IMMUTABLE`), `privacy-facts.md` §2); the takedown cannot reach it. |
| Cover fetched from Open Library | instance admin | The same. The hash block also stops the server from fetching it again. |
| Profile picture | instance admin; the user | From the report: "Take down", as for a cover; the queue shows the picture and whose it is. A picture its owner replaced or deleted while it was reported is gone (the app keeps no copy): the queue says "No longer online", offers only "Close the report", and the server refuses a takedown ("The reported picture is no longer online"); closing it closes every open report of that owner's gone pictures, and the reporters learn that it is no longer online. Such a picture could come back with the same file, as no takedown blocked its hash (to confirm: keeping a reported picture until the decision needs a change in `server/src/media/avatar.rs`, another package's file). Without a report: the "Take down a picture by URL" form with the picture's address (`/media/{public_id}/a`), or `DELETE /api/admin/media/{public_id}`, with or without a notice. The picture then shows nowhere (every place that shows a profile picture skips a removed one), `/media/{public_id}/a` answers 410, and the same file cannot be uploaded again. The user removes their own with `DELETE /api/auth/me/avatar`. |
| Book or edition a reader added (title, authors, description, details) | instance admin | From the report: "Remove the text", then "Remove for good", withdraws what the reader typed and keeps the record, so the shelves, posts and picks that point at it keep working: a book gets the title `[removed]` and loses its subtitle, authors, description and subjects; an edition loses its own title, subtitle and publisher and shows its book's title (`server/src/catalog/reports.rs` (`decide`, `REMOVED_TITLE`)). The fields get provenance `admin`, so no source fills them again. A book merged into another one since it was reported (for example a reader's duplicate of an Open Library book) no longer shows on its own: the queue shows the book it was merged into with "Merged into this book since it was reported" and offers only "Close the report", and the server refuses "remove" ("The reported book was merged into another one"), so a removal never reaches the other book or another reader's. If the other book took over some of the reported text (a merge fills its empty fields), correct it with "Edit as admin", then close the report. To correct rather than remove: "Edit as admin" (`frontend/src/components/EditRequestSheet.vue`), `PATCH /api/admin/works/{work_id}` and `PATCH /api/admin/editions/{edition_id}`; a reader's edit request approved with `POST /api/admin/edit-requests/{request_id}/approve`; a duplicate merged away with `POST /api/admin/works/{work_id}/merge` (`frontend/src/components/admin/AdminRequests.vue`). The book's cover is a picture of its own: report and remove it as above. |
| Description from Open Library | instance admin | Q18 option A: `PATCH /api/admin/works/{work_id}` clears or replaces it, and a later refresh leaves the admin's value alone. |
| Club post or reply | the club's admins and owner; the author | The delete action on the post, `DELETE /api/posts/{post_id}` (`server/src/posts/edit.rs:178-190`). A post others replied to becomes a tombstone without text. The instance admin has no in-app way (Q11 option C adds reports on posts with #57); until then, the operator asks the club's admins, or deletes the text by hand in the database (to confirm the procedure). |
| Personal diary entry, quote, rating take | the author | `DELETE /api/posts/{post_id}`, `DELETE /api/quotes/{quote_id}`, `PATCH /api/shelf/{entry_id}`. The instance admin has no in-app way (as above). |
| Club name | the club's owner and admins | `PATCH /api/clubs/{slug}`. The instance admin: by hand (to confirm). |
| Username | nobody in the app | Usernames cannot be changed. The operator renames by hand, or suspends the account (`POST /api/admin/users/{username}/suspend`). |
| Account | the user; the operator | The user deletes it in Settings (`frontend/src/components/DeleteAccount.vue`, `DELETE /api/auth/me`). The instance admin suspends it (Q15): `POST /api/admin/users/{username}/suspend`, undone with `POST /api/admin/users/{username}/unsuspend`, and `GET /api/admin/users/suspended` lists the suspended accounts (`server/src/admin/users.rs` (`suspend`, `unsuspend`, `list_suspended`)). A suspended account cannot sign in (login answers "This account is suspended" once the password matched), every session and app token it had ends at once, and its content stays. An admin cannot suspend their own account. On the admin page: the "Accounts" tab (`frontend/src/components/admin/AdminUsers.vue`) suspends by username after a second tap, lists the suspended accounts with the time of the suspension, and lifts one with "Lift suspension". |

"By hand" means the operator changes the database directly. Every such change is written down in the record (§6).

## 5. Decision, statement of reasons, complaints

- **Decision.** The admin decides "remove" or "dismiss" (`POST /api/admin/reports/{report_id}/resolve`): "Take down" (a picture) or "Remove the text" (a book or edition), each confirmed with a second tap, or "Keep it". Either way every open report of the same picture, book or edition is resolved with it, and "Decided" in the reports tab lists the decision with the admin and the time it took.
- **Reporter.** Gets the decision (Art. 16 (5) DSA): after the decision commits, the app mails each reporter of the resolved reports whether the content was removed or kept, or, for content gone before the decision, that it is no longer online (a profile picture its owner replaced or deleted) or was merged with another entry of the same book, in their language, to their verified address (`server/src/email/templates.rs` (`report_decision`, `Outcome`)). The mail names nothing about the uploader. A kept report tells the reporter where to object (`CONTACT_EMAIL`). A decision that fails or is rolled back sends nothing. An account without a verified address gets no mail; notices by mail are answered by hand, from the contact mailbox.
- **Uploader.** Gets a statement of reasons when their content is removed (Art. 17 DSA): the uploader of a cover, the owner of a profile picture, the reader who added a book or edition (`server/src/email/templates.rs` (`statement_of_reasons`)). It says what was removed and what changes, the ground (the reason the report or notice gave, or that an admin found it unlawful or against the terms when it was removed without one), that a person decided and no automated system, how to object (`CONTACT_EMAIL`), and links the terms. It never names the reporter or quotes the report. A dismissal, or a "remove" of something removed before, sends no statement. Covers from Open Library have no uploader. To a verified address only; for an account without one, the operator decides whether to write by hand.
- **Objection.** The uploader or the reporter writes to `CONTACT_EMAIL`; the operator reviews the decision and answers within the same target (to confirm). Whether the internal complaint system of Art. 20 DSA applies (it applies to online platforms, and content inside closed clubs is not disseminated to the public) is for the lawyer to confirm.
- **Repeat abuse.** Suspension (Q15, §4; the "Accounts" tab) after a warning, per the terms (Art. 23 DSA, if it applies; to confirm). The app sends no mail on a suspension: the operator writes the statement of reasons for it by hand from the contact mailbox (Art. 17 DSA also covers suspensions; to confirm).

## 6. Records

- **In the database.** Every report keeps what was reported (a cover, a profile picture with whose it was, a book or an edition), the reason, details, reporter, how it came in (`via`: `app` or `notice`), the time, the deciding admin, the time and the outcome, and whether it was closed on arrival (`reports`, `server/migrations/0001_init.sql`, `server/migrations/2101_reports_targets.sql`). A takedown also sets `media.removed_at` and keeps the blocked hashes (`media_removed_hashes`). A direct takedown with a body records a resolved report with the time the notice arrived. Edit requests keep the deciding admin, the time and the note (`catalog_edit_requests`). A suspension keeps its time (`users.suspended_at`, `server/migrations/2102_users_suspended_at.sql`); who suspended and why go into the mailbox record.
- **In the mailbox.** Notices received by mail, acknowledgements, decisions sent by hand, objections and answers, suspensions with their reasons, and every change made by hand in the database (what, when, why). Keep them in one folder of the contact mailbox.
- **Retention.** Reports and decided catalog requests are deleted 3 years after the decision by the hourly cleanup (`server/src/auth/mod.rs` (`cleanup`)), the photo of a rejected cover request with its request (`delete_rejected_cover_photos`); open reports and requests are kept until decided (Q14, `privacy-facts.md` §7). The mails in the contact mailbox: 3 years after the decision (to confirm).
- **Export.** Reports a user filed and resolved are part of their export, and so are the removals of their own content after a report (their profile picture, pictures they uploaded, books and editions they added), one entry per decision with its reason and date (`reports` section with `filed`, `resolved` and `about_me`, `docs/phase1/conventions.md` §5.7). Open and dismissed reports of their content, how many reports a decision closed, how and when they came in, who filed them and what they wrote are withheld: only club mates can report a profile picture, so in a club of 2 to 5 any of these can name the reporter (Art. 15 (4) GDPR, § 29 (1) BDSG; security review, Phase 2 Wave 2). The statement of reasons already told the owner of every removal.

## 7. Accounts that cannot sign in

A suspended account loses the in-app export and deletion (DSGVO Art. 15, 17 and 20; work plan amendment 20): it cannot sign in, so it reaches neither Settings nor `GET /api/auth/me/export` nor `DELETE /api/auth/me`. The "Accounts" tab says so beside the list. Its owner writes to `CONTACT_EMAIL`. The operator:

1. Checks that the request comes from the account's verified address: in `psql` on the instance (`docker compose exec postgres psql -U bookclub bookclub`), `SELECT username, email, email_verified_at, suspended_at FROM users WHERE username = '<name>';`. A request from another address is answered with a request to write from that one.
2. **Export.** Downloads it for them. The export needs a live session, which a suspended account cannot have, so the account is unsuspended for these steps: **from step 2.1 until step 2.4 it is usable**, and whoever knows its password can sign in and post. Run 2.1 to 2.4 back to back, in one sitting of a few minutes, and check afterwards that the "Accounts" tab lists the account again. Step 2.4 ends every session made meanwhile. (To confirm: an operator command that builds the export without a session, such as `bookclub-server export-user <name>`, would need no unsuspension; the server has none yet.)
   1. Unsuspend it: "Lift suspension" in the "Accounts" tab, or `POST /api/admin/users/{username}/unsuspend` (the account still has no session: the suspension ended them all).
   2. In `psql`, make a one-off app token that lives ten minutes and print it:
      ```sql
      WITH s AS (SELECT decode(replace(gen_random_uuid()::text || gen_random_uuid()::text, '-', ''), 'hex') AS secret)
      INSERT INTO sessions (user_id, kind, token_hash, label, expires_at)
      SELECT u.id, 'bearer', sha256(s.secret), 'operator export', now() + interval '10 minutes'
      FROM users u, s WHERE u.username = '<name>'
      RETURNING 'bc_' || rtrim(translate(encode((SELECT secret FROM s), 'base64'), '+/', '-_'), '=') AS token;
      ```
   3. `curl -fsS -H "Authorization: Bearer <token>" -o export.zip https://<host>/api/auth/me/export`
   4. Suspend it again: "Suspend" in the "Accounts" tab, or `POST /api/admin/users/{username}/suspend`, which deletes the token.
   5. Send `export.zip` to the verified address (as an encrypted archive where possible), delete the local copy, and note the request and the answer in the record (§6).
3. **Deletion.** The deletion asks for the account's password, which only its owner knows, so the owner deletes it in Settings while the account is unsuspended. **Risk: while it is unsuspended the account is fully usable**, so a suspended abuser regains access just by asking for deletion. To keep that window short: agree a time with the owner (at the verified address), unsuspend the account then, and suspend it again after an hour if it still exists. An owner of clubs first transfers or deletes them (the deletion answers "Transfer or delete your clubs first"). Note it in the record. (To confirm with the legal source: whether this window is acceptable, or the operator deletes the account without the owner's password; that needs an operator command through `server/src/auth/deletion.rs`, which the server does not have yet.)
