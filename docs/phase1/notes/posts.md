## How reads work

- **One loader.** Every endpoint first selects post ids (`JOIN bc_visible_posts($viewer)`), then `post_outs` loads the rows again through `bc_visible_posts`, so a caller can never hand it an id the viewer may not see. Threads keep tombstones as anchors; the feed adds `deleted_at IS NULL`.
- **Work thread** (`GET /api/books/works/{work_id}/posts`): roots `WHERE work_id = $w AND parent_id IS NULL`, then their visible replies, both ordered `created_at, id`. That is the viewer's visible diary entries plus the club posts of the viewer's clubs (each with `club`).
- **Pick threads:** the club comes from `ClubMember`; the pick is `WHERE club_id = $club AND (ended_at IS NULL | id = $pick)`; roots `WHERE club_id = $club AND pick_id = $pick AND parent_id IS NULL`, with replies. `can_post` = the pick has not ended. `timezone` = the club's zone.
- **Feed** (`GET /api/diary`): keyset on `id DESC` with `limit + 1`. `?club=<slug>` goes through `clubs::extract::member_by_slug` (404 `No such club`) and keeps that club's posts plus diary posts (roots and replies) whose author is a **current** member of the club. An empty `club=` is no filter. Each item carries `book` (`BookOut` without an edition context), the viewer's `my_progress`/`my_status` on that work, `club` (the same `ClubRef` as `entry.club`) and `parent_author`.
- **`author_rating`:** `bc_visible_shelf(viewer)` row of the author for the post's work with status `finished`, so it is live, null unless finished, and null for someone else's `only_me` rating (also null for a tombstone of a deleted account).
- **Reactions:** `count` counts every row, `mine` is exact, `users` lists the names in `bc_visible_owners(viewer)` (the viewer and everyone sharing a club with them, which includes the members of a club post's club), sorted by byte order. Groups are sorted by count descending, then emoji by code point (spec R12). Tombstones show `[]`.
- **`parent_author`:** the parent's author's username when that user is in `bc_visible_owners(viewer)`, else null. A diary reply the viewer can see always has a visible parent author, except under the tombstone of a deleted account (null). For a club reply whose parent's author left the club and shares no other club with the viewer, it is null too, although the club's own thread still names that author (club posts are club data).
- **`created_label`:** a club post's label is in **its club's** zone wherever it appears (pick thread, work thread, feed, PATCH and reaction answers); a diary entry's label is in the **viewer's** zone. `DiaryOut.timezone`/`DiaryFeedOut.timezone` are the viewer's zone, `PickThreadOut.timezone` the club's. So in the work thread and the feed, a club post's label can be in another zone than the response's `timezone`. This follows work-plan appendix diary 11 and club 4 and **conflicts with the conventions §12 table** (see deviation 4).

## How writes work

- **Order of checks, diary POST:** auth → body validation (400, all messages in field order) → `{work_id}` (`WorkRef::parse` + `resolve_local`: numeric id, `OL…W`, `BC…`/`ISBN…`/`GB…` legacy keys; a merged work resolves to its target; anything else 404 `No such book.`) → for an entry: the work lock (`write::lock_work`); for a reply: the parent (below, which takes the work lock after the parent's club) → insert.
- **Order of checks, pick POST:** auth and membership (404 `No such club`) → path (400 non-numeric pick id) → body (400) → the club lock and the membership again (404 `No such club`) → the pick (404 `No club pick yet` / `No club pick with that id`, another club's pick included) → `That pick is closed` → the pick's work lock (the pick's `work_id` read again under it) → `That milestone is not on this pick` (`milestones WHERE club_id AND pick_id AND id FOR KEY SHARE`) → the parent → insert.
- **Parent of a reply** (`write::reply_parent`): `visibility::post_access(…, allow_tombstone = true)` (a 404 becomes 400 `That entry is not on this book`), the parent's club row `FOR KEY SHARE`, the thread's work (`lock_work`), `post_access` again under those locks (a club parent also needs `club_role`), then `SELECT … FOR KEY SHARE` on the parent, then, in the legacy order: not on this thread (another work than the locked one; on the pick routes also another club or pick) → 400 `That entry is not on this book`; a reply → 400 `Reply to the entry itself, not to a reply`; a tombstone → 400 `That entry was deleted`. The reply copies the parent's `club_id` and `pick_id` (`posts_parent_fk`).
- **Insert** (`write::insert_post`): snapshot of the author's own shelf position (`position`), then one `INSERT … SELECT … WHERE club_id IS NULL OR EXISTS (open pick)`; zero rows is 400 `That pick is closed` (a pick that ends while the request runs gets no new post). The milestone is checked under `FOR KEY SHARE` before the insert: a delete in flight is waited for and then answers `That milestone is not on this pick` (an unlocked check passed it and the insert's foreign key then failed with a 500), and a delete that starts later waits for the post and then detaches it (`ON DELETE SET NULL`). Every club post records `note_posted` `{post_id}` (club, actor, work, pick).
- **PATCH:** `post_access(…, false)` (404) → author only (403 `You can only edit your own entries`, also for `{}`) → `Nothing to update` (body null, `spoiler_upto` absent, `visibility` null) → `only_me` on a reply or club post (400 `Replies and club posts cannot be private`) → `UPDATE … WHERE id AND author_id AND deleted_at IS NULL` with `updated_at = now()` always (spec R8); zero rows is 404. Answers `PostOut` with replies.
- **DELETE:** `post_access(…, false)` → the author, or `club_role >= Admin` **of that post's club** on a club post (moderation), else 403 `You can only delete your own entries` → `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on the post (404 if it went meanwhile) → delete its reactions → a post with replies becomes a tombstone (`body ''`, `spoiler_upto NULL`, `deleted_at now()`; `progress_at`, `status_at`, `updated_at`, `pick_id`, `milestone_id` and `created_at` stay), any other is deleted. No event.
- **Reactions:** body validation (the stripped emoji must be one of the six, 400 `Pick one of the club reactions`) → `write::access_in_lock_order` (`post_access(…, false)`, the club row, the post's work, `post_access` again) → `SELECT … FOR KEY SHARE` (404 if deleted meanwhile) → `DELETE … RETURNING`; if nothing was deleted, `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING RETURNING`. Only a stored insert on a club post records `reaction` `{emoji, post_id, author_id}` (club, actor, work, pick). Diary reactions record nothing (parity). Answers `PostOut` with replies.
- **Lock order: club row, then work, then post.** A club delete, a removal and an account deletion lock the club rows `FOR UPDATE` before they reach posts (conventions §5.3, §5.6). So every club write here takes the club row `FOR KEY SHARE` first: a new pick post through `write::lock_club_member` (which then checks the membership again in a statement of its own: 404 `No such club` when the club was deleted or the caller removed while the request waited), a reply or a reaction on a club post through `write::reply_parent` / `write::access_in_lock_order` (`post_access`, the club lock, the work lock, `post_access` again). Only then is the post locked. Locking the post first and needing the club row later for the foreign keys of the insert and the event deadlocked with a club delete (`tests/posts_races.rs::a_reaction_on_a_club_post_waits_for_a_club_delete` and `::a_reply_to_a_club_post_waits_for_a_club_delete` fail on the old order). A member removed while a reply or reaction waited is refused by the second `post_access` (`::a_reply_to_a_club_post_waits_for_a_removal`: 400; `::a_reaction_on_a_club_post_waits_for_a_removal`: 404, nothing stored. For the reaction the answer's own `post_out`, read in the same transaction, would refuse it too and roll the toggle back).
- **Work lock (CAT's rule for writes that store a `work_id`, docs/phase1/notes/cat.md "For SHELF, POSTS and CLUB").** Every post insert and every reaction locks its work with `write::lock_work`: `SELECT id FROM works WHERE id = $1 AND merged_into_id IS NULL FOR SHARE`, and when no row comes back it follows `merged_into_id` (`catalog::shared::books::require_work`) and locks again. It comes after the club row and before the post, the milestone and (through the foreign keys) the user rows, matching `merge_works`' works → users → posts. The lock conflicts with the merge's `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, so a write waits for a merge in flight and lands on the winner, and a merge that starts later waits for the write and moves its row. What follows the lock is read again under it: the pick's `work_id` on the pick routes, the post's `work_id` for a reaction (its `reaction` event names the winner), and the parent on the diary route (it must be on the locked work). Without the lock a post written during a merge stayed on the merged-away work, outside every thread; a pick post could land on the loser while its pick moved, so replies to it were refused; and a reply or reaction that held the post while the merge held the user row deadlocked with it (40P01, a 500 for one side). `tests/posts_races.rs::an_entry_written_during_a_merge_lands_on_the_winner`, `::a_reply_written_during_a_merge_follows_its_parent` and `::a_pick_post_written_during_a_merge_follows_its_pick` run CAT's `merge_works` in a transaction they hold open, and `::a_reaction_during_a_merge_waits_instead_of_deadlocking` replays its steps (the works, then the user rows, then `UPDATE posts`) so that the reaction starts between them; all four fail without the lock. An edit and a delete store no `work_id` and take no work lock: they hold the post, which the merge's `UPDATE posts` waits for, and need nothing the merge holds (no foreign key of theirs changes).
- **Residual race with account deletion:** an account deletion (W1) locks the user and their posts, and then `DELETE FROM users` sets `works.created_by` to NULL on the works the user added, which needs those works `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`. A reply or reaction by someone else to the deleting user's **diary** post on a work **that user added** holds the work `FOR SHARE` and then waits for the post, so the two can deadlock (40P01: Postgres aborts one, a 500 with nothing changed; the user repeats it). The window needs all three at once and is accepted: the deletion's order (users → works) and the merge's (works → users) disagree, so no order of the work lock suits both, and CAT's rule takes precedence. Club posts are not affected: the deletion locks the post's club first, where the reply or reaction waits.
- **Post locks:** a reply and a reaction hold the post `FOR KEY SHARE`, a delete takes it `FOR UPDATE`. A delete therefore waits for a reply in flight and leaves a tombstone, and a reply that waited for a delete gets `That entry was deleted` or `That entry is not on this book`, never a 500 (`tests/posts_races.rs`, deterministic with a held lock; removing the reply's lock makes `a_reply_waits_for_a_delete_in_flight` fail). Account deletion (W1) locks the user's posts `FOR UPDATE` after the user row, so the same guarantees hold against it. A delete and an edit need no club lock: neither touches a foreign key to the club.

## Deviations and decisions (beyond endpoint-map §8)

1. **New 400 string `Replies and club posts cannot be private`.** `POST /api/books/works/{work_id}/posts` with `parent_id` and `"visibility": "only_me"` is a validation error; `PATCH /api/posts/{post_id}` with `"visibility": "only_me"` on a reply or a club post is refused after the 403/`Nothing to update` checks. `"audience"` is accepted there (a no-op that still bumps `edited`). The schema CHECK `posts_visibility_scope` would otherwise answer 500.
2. **`note_posted` for every new club post**, replies included, whichever route created them (the pick routes, or the diary route replying to a club post). Endpoint-map's "No event (parity)" on the diary route holds for diary entries, which record nothing.
3. **Club replies.** Replies are allowed on the pick routes (the parent must be a root of the same pick in the same club) and through the diary route (the parent's scope wins; membership of the parent's club is required through `post_access.club_role`). A new club post, reply or not, on a pick that has ended is 400 `That pick is closed`; edits, deletes and reactions on such posts stay allowed (parity: no closed check).
4. **`created_label` zones: conflicts with conventions §12.** A club post's label is in its club's zone everywhere (pick thread, work thread, `/api/diary`, PATCH and reaction answers); a diary entry's label is in the viewer's zone. This follows work-plan appendix diary 11 ("the club's zone for club labels, the viewer's zone for personal ones") and club 4 ("Home-feed labels use the item's club zone for club items"), and keeps a club post's label the same wherever it appears. The conventions §12 "Which zone applies" table says instead that `created_label` on diary and home-feed items uses the viewer's zone, and keeps club zones for "club feed and thread labels". **For the integrator:** amend the §12 row to "`created_label` on diary and home-feed items: the viewer's zone for personal items, the item's club zone for club items", or tell POSTS to switch the work thread and `/api/diary` to the viewer's zone (one line in `out::post_outs` plus `tests/posts_picks.rs::post_times_use_the_club_timezone`). As built, a club post's label in the work thread and the feed can be in another zone than the response's `timezone` (the viewer's). `tests/posts_picks.rs::post_times_use_the_club_timezone` asserts the club zone in the pick thread, the work thread and the feed.
5. **`parent_author`** follows `bc_visible_owners`, as described above.
6. **`DiaryOut`** is `{work_id, my_progress, my_status, items, timezone}`: the legacy `ol_work_key` and `book_id` are gone (an unknown work is a 404 now, so `book_id` would never be null).
7. **The feed's `?club=`** has no `joined_at` cut-off (unlike the activity feed): a new member's older diary entries show in the club's feed. A member's diary replies count as diary entries.
8. **`spoiler_upto`** is parsed as a 64-bit integer, so `101`, `-1` and huge values all get pydantic's range message instead of `Invalid value`; the OpenAPI type is `int64`.
9. **Moderation** reads the role from `post_access.club_role` inside the request's transaction; it is not re-checked under a club lock (a post delete is not a membership write, conventions §5.3). New club posts, club replies and club reactions do take the club row `FOR KEY SHARE` first (see "Lock order"), so they wait for a membership write or a club delete in that club.
10. **Path ids:** `{post_id}` and `{pick_id}` are `ApiPath<i64>` (non-numeric 400, overflow 404 `Not found`); `{work_id}` is a string resolved as above.

## For FE (#48)

- `PostOut` replaces `DiaryEntryOut` and `PickPostOut`; edits, deletes and reactions of pick posts go to `/api/posts/{post_id}…`. `author` is null only on the tombstone of a deleted account; `author_avatar_url` is the `/media/…/a` URL.
- `DiaryOut` has `work_id` instead of `ol_work_key`/`book_id`.
- `reactions[].users` may hold fewer names than `count` (people outside the viewer's audience are counted, not named).
- `parent_author` may be null on a reply (deleted or not visible author).
- A club post's `created_label` is in its club's zone even in the work thread and the feed, so it can differ from the response's `timezone` (the viewer's). When formatting `created_at` yourself, use the zone of the post's `club` (from `UserOut.clubs[].timezone`) for a club post and the response's `timezone` for a diary entry. This conflicts with conventions §12 and may change (deviation 4).
- `visibility` is only meaningful on diary roots; the composer should offer it only there.
- Club threads allow replies now (`parent_id` on `POST /api/clubs/{slug}/pick/posts`).

## For other packages

- **CLUB:** `note_posted` is `{post_id}` and `reaction` is `{emoji, post_id, author_id}` with `club_id`, `work_id` and `pick_id` set; the feed resolves `author_id`. A milestone's `note_count` is `count(*) FROM posts WHERE milestone_id = $id` (club posts; replies may carry a milestone too). Deleting a milestone needs no detach step: `posts_milestone_fk` sets `milestone_id` to NULL. A post naming a milestone holds it `FOR KEY SHARE` until it commits, so a milestone delete waits for a post in flight (and a post waits for a delete in flight and is then refused). Ending or changing a pick touches no post; its thread stays readable by id.
- **CAT:** POSTS follows the work-lock rule of "For SHELF, POSTS and CLUB" (see "Work lock" above). `phase1/posts` has merged `phase1-green` (with CAT); POSTS calls `catalog::shared::books::require_work`, and its race tests call `catalog::merge_works`.
- **IMP:** posts written by the importer are read the same way (`bc_visible_posts`); a club post needs `club_id` and `pick_id` set together, and a reply copies its parent's scope.
